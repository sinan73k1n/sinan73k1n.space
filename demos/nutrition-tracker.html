Meal calculator

Pick items from the library, say how much of each went into the plate, and the total comes out — calories and macros. One formula does all of it: amount × kcal per 100 ÷ 100. Save the result back as a dish and you never calculate that plate again.

Food library

Two kinds of entry, one idea: calorie density. A product stores calories per 100 g/ml straight off the label. A dish stores what a whole plate weighs and what it costs — the app divides it down. The calculator treats both the same way.

Editing:

Copy the “per 100 g / 100 ml” numbers from the label. Macros are optional — leave them empty and only calories are tracked.

Daily budget

Everything else on this page hangs off these four numbers. Change any input and watch the whole chain recalculate — the steps below are written out with the actual figures, so nothing here is a black box.

How the number was reached

Mifflin-St Jeor equation → activity factor → energy deficit. Every line shows the arithmetic with your inputs substituted in.

Macro targets

Protein and fat are set per kilogram of goal weight; carbohydrate takes whatever calories are left. 1 g protein = 4 kcal, 1 g carbs = 4 kcal, 1 g fat = 9 kcal.
